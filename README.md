# Kasato-maru TEI corpus (1908)

Data and scripts for: Andrew Lao, "Naming the New World: Orthographic Instability and Categories of Belonging in the Kasato-maru Texts of 1908" (submitted to the Journal of the Japanese Association for Digital Humanities).

## Contents

- `authority.xml`: TEI stand-off records for places, persons, organisations, groups, contact vocabulary and events.
- `tables.txt`: Tables 1–4 and the voyage check, as generated by `make_tables.py`.
- Scripts, in the order they were run:
  1. `make_skeleton.py`: builds the diary's 128 dated entries from the raw text
  2. `add_sections.py`: adds the NDL's four dated sections
  3. `encode_voyage.py`: weather, noon positions and distances (voyage)
  4. `encode_shore.py`: weather and currency (shore entries)
  5. `encode_editorial.py`: the NDL's editorial signs (sic, supplied, gap, ruby, abbreviation)
  6. `encode_names.py`: places, persons, organisations, terms and group labels
  7. `update_header.py`: the diary's TEI header
  8. `build_uetsuka.py`: builds the encoding of Uetsuka's two dispatches
  9. `make_tables.py`: generates the paper's tables from the encoded files

## Source texts

The encoded texts (`mizuno.xml`, `uetsuka.xml`) are not yet included. They are based on transcriptions published by the National Diet Library (NDL), and will be added once the NDL's reuse terms are confirmed.

To rebuild them, copy the text of these NDL pages into a folder named `raw`:

- https://www.ndl.go.jp/brasil/text/t102.html → `raw/mizuno_ndl_raw.txt`
- https://www.ndl.go.jp/brasil/text/t103.html → `raw/uetsuka_ndl_raw.txt`

Then run the scripts in the order above with Python 3.
